Pricing FAQ

Questions About Our Plans

Can I upgrade my plan mid-year?
Absolutely! You can upgrade at any time and we'll prorate the difference. Contact us to discuss upgrading your plan — our team will make the transition seamless.
Are installation costs separate from service plans?
Yes, service plans cover ongoing maintenance and inspections. New installation costs are quoted separately based on the specific fireplace model and project scope. However, plan members receive exclusive discounts on installation services.
Do you offer financing options?
Yes, we partner with leading financing providers to offer flexible payment plans for both new installations and service plans. Options include 0% APR for 12 months on qualifying purchases. Ask us for details during your consultation.
