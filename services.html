FAQ

Common Questions

Everything you need to know about our installation and maintenance services.

How long does a typical fireplace installation take?
Most standard gas or electric fireplace installations are completed in 1–2 days. Wood-burning fireplaces with new chimneys may take 3–5 days. Custom mantel and surround installations typically add 1–2 additional days. We'll provide a detailed timeline during your consultation.
Do you handle permits and inspections?
Yes, we handle all necessary permits and coordinate with local building inspectors. Our team is fully licensed and ensures every installation meets current code requirements. Permit costs are included in our installation quotes.
How often should I service my fireplace?
We recommend annual maintenance for all fireplace types. Gas fireplaces should be inspected and serviced once a year. Wood-burning fireplaces and chimneys should be inspected annually and swept when creosote buildup exceeds 1/8 inch. Electric fireplaces need periodic cleaning and inspection.
Can I convert my wood fireplace to gas?
Yes! Gas inserts are specifically designed to fit inside existing wood-burning fireplaces. The conversion typically takes one day and dramatically improves efficiency — from around 10% with an open wood fireplace to 70–85% with a modern gas insert.
What warranty do you offer on installations?
All our installations come with a 5-year labor warranty and the full manufacturer warranty on products (typically 5–25 years depending on the manufacturer). Our maintenance plan customers receive extended coverage and priority scheduling.
